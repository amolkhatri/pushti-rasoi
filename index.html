Good to know

Eggless homemade cookies · Nanded City, Pune

Are your cookies eggless?

Yes. Every Pushti Rasoi cookie is 100% eggless.

What are the cookies made of?

Whole wheat flour and almond flour, pure cow ghee, and jaggery. We use no maida (refined flour) and no refined sugar.

Are they sugar-free?

No. They are sweetened with jaggery instead of refined white sugar, so they still contain natural sugar.

Where are you located?

Our home kitchen is in Nanded City, Pune. We confirm the pick-up time and share the exact location on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver nearby: Nanded City, Sinhagad Road, Dhayari, Narhe, Khadakwasla and Warje. Choose home delivery when you pre-order.

How do I order?

Add packs from the menu, fill in your details and tap Pre-order on WhatsApp. Please order 1–2 days ahead so we can bake fresh.

How big is a pack?

Each pack is 250 g, about 60 small coin cookies. Prices start at ₹299.
