The Cookie Story

1

The Idea

We wanted a cookie that feels like a treat but is made the way we cook at home. So we swapped maida for whole wheat and almond flour, and used jaggery and pure cow ghee.

2

The Dough

The flours, cow ghee and jaggery are mixed into a soft dough, the choco chips and flavours are folded in, and the dough is shaped into tiny coins.

3

Baking & Packing

The coins are baked fresh in small batches, only after you order. Finally they're packed with love in 250 g packs, ready for you to pick up.

Good to know

Eggless homemade cookies · Nanded City, Pune

Are your cookies eggless?

Yes. Every Pushti Rasoi cookie is 100% eggless.

What are the cookies made of?

Whole wheat flour and almond flour, pure cow ghee and jaggery. We use no maida (refined flour).

Are they sugar-free?

No. The dough is sweetened with jaggery instead of refined white sugar, and add-ins like choco chips, white chocolate and Oreo contain regular sugar.

Where are you located?

Our home kitchen is in Nanded City, Pune. We confirm the pick-up time and share the exact location on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver?

Usually not. Most orders are pick up from our home kitchen in Nanded City. For larger orders we can arrange home delivery nearby (Nanded City, Sinhagad Road, Dhayari, Narhe, Khadakwasla, Warje). Message us on WhatsApp to check.

How do I order?

Add packs from the menu, fill in your details and tap Pre-order on WhatsApp. Please order 1–2 days ahead so we can bake fresh.
